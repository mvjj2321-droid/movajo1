CMS Guide

Adding Movies

1
Click the + button in the nav bar
2
Choose "Paste Link" and enter your Google Drive share link or any direct video URL
3
Fill in title, genre, year, and optionally a poster image URL
4
Click "Add to Library" — it appears instantly in your collection

Editing Thumbnails

1
Click any title to open its detail view
2
Tap the 🖼️ icon to open the thumbnail editor
3
Paste a new image URL or use 🔍 Search to find posters automatically

Cloud Sync (Cross-Device)

1
Go to Profile → Cloud Sync Setup
2
Create a free Firebase project at console.firebase.google.com
3
Add a Web App → copy API Key, Auth Domain, and Project ID
4
Enable Firestore Database (start in test mode for 30 days)
5
Paste credentials in Movajo → your library syncs instantly across all devices

TV Shows

1
Use + → TV Show to create a show entry
2
Add seasons and paste episode Drive links individually
3
The show appears in the TV Shows tab with full episode navigation
